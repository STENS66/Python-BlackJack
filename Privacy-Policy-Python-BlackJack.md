# Privacy Policy - Python-BlackJack

Privacy policy for the **Python-BlackJack** application.

## **Data Collection**

Our application **Python-BlackJack** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to remote servers or over the Internet. All information entered or generated by the user remains exclusively on the local device where the application is installed.

## **Virtual Currency and Simulated Gambling Disclaimer**

**Python-BlackJack** is a card simulation game intended **solely for entertainment and leisure purposes**.

* The application features virtual chips and simulated betting mechanics.

* **No Real Money:** No real money, currency, or items of monetary value are used, won, or lost within the game.

* The virtual currency has **no real-world value** and **cannot be exchanged for cash, goods, or any other tangible assets**. No financial transactions, deposits, withdrawals, or actual gambling activities take place within the application.

## **Use of Data**

Any data or game state generated by the user is used exclusively for **local processing** on the user's device. It is stored locally and is never shared with third parties.

## **Data Sharing**

We **do not share** any user data with third parties. All data remains strictly localized on the user's device. Having no access to the data entered, played, or saved via this application, users are solely responsible for managing and preserving their own game progress and local settings.

## **Data Security**

During the development of this application, we implemented all necessary measures to ensure that any data stored remains solely on the user's device. These measures include the protections offered by the operating system and standard Microsoft security features. Additionally, when downloading the application or visiting our repository pages, **no personal data is collected by default**.

## **User Rights**

In accordance with applicable regulations, you have the right to:

* **Access** the data stored locally by the application on your device.

* **Rectify or erase** your local application data (which can be easily achieved by clearing the app data or uninstalling it).

Please note that all data remains exclusively on your device and is **never transmitted to or collected by our services**.

## **Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
