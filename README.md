## Description

**Python-BlackJack** is a fast, high-performance, and **100% offline** Blackjack game. Engineered from the ground up for UWP (Universal Windows Platform), it brings the classic casino experience to your Windows PC. Enjoy a completely local game with beautiful UI and immersive sounds, all while ensuring absolute privacy.

The application is exclusively developed in **C# and XAML** (despite its historical name) and utilizes a robust architecture for smooth state transitions and fixed-resolution scaling. Validated and published on the **Microsoft Store**, it provides a robust, seamless, and completely isolated closed-loop environment to enjoy Blackjack locally with zero risk of data leakage.

### Usage

To play **"Python-BlackJack"**:

**1. Launch the Game:**

- Open the application on your PC.

**2. Place Your Bets:**

- Use your mouse, touch screen, or Xbox controller to select your chips and place your bets.

**3. Play the Hand:**

- Hit, Stand, Double Down, or take Insurance based on your hand and the dealer's visible card.

**4. Win and Repeat:**

- The game automatically resolves the hand, calculates payouts, and resets for the next round.
